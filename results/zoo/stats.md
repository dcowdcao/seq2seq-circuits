# Zoo statistics

Generated by `python results/zoo/zoo_stats.py` from `models_index.csv`, `circuits_index.csv` and `probes_index.csv` (built by `build_index.py` from the pipeline artifacts). Do not edit by hand.

The run-list coverage table also reads `pipeline/models/models.txt`, and the BridgeCircuit note reads the showcase BridgeCircuit's `site_scan.json`.

## About this snapshot

The circuit records come from two discovery runs of the pipeline (`run` column of `circuits_index.csv`):

- **early** run (20,000 test rows per circuit): 216 rows (DomainCircuit 136, ContrastCircuit 80); floors DomainCircuit 0.90, ContrastCircuit 0.90.
- **main** run (5,000 test rows per circuit): 3,822 rows (DomainCircuit 2,021, ContrastCircuit 1,401, SuppressionCircuit 400); floors DomainCircuit 0.85, ContrastCircuit 0.85, SuppressionCircuit 0.50.
- 23 further rows are markers written without an evaluation (e.g. the base circuit had no faithful winner); they carry no run.

Every verdict is the zoo-wide selection of `pipeline/find_circuits/smallest_faithful_circuit.py`, under edge-level per-position mean ablation:

- **DomainCircuit**: absolute whole-sequence accuracy of the circuit ≥ the row's floor, plus the stage's other eligibility conditions and its winner rule (docs/circuits.md §6.2; each record states the `selection_regime` it was selected under).
- **ContrastCircuit**: differential mode: `s_union` (whole-sequence accuracy of candidate ∪ DomainCircuit) ≥ the row's floor, with a non-empty fringe.
- **SuppressionCircuit**: severing the fringe releases the wrong task (release ≥ 0.5) with collateral ≤ 0.05, under the cued-row or the wrong-cue (tonic) sever reference; all 125 faithful SuppressionCircuits pass only via the wrong-cue (tonic) reference.

These are not the per-class faithfulness definitions and floors, nor the interchange patching for ContrastCircuits, used for the showcase circuits (see [docs/circuits.md](../../docs/circuits.md)); numbers from the two are not comparable. Edge fractions below use the live-edge denominator.

8 checkpoints score below the 0.95 eval sequence-accuracy gate; they and their circuits are included in every table. 141 circuit rows (58 models) have no checkpoint in the indexed set; they are included in the counts and faithful rates, and their full node/edge budgets use the architecture's default geometry.

All 60 BridgeCircuit rows of `probes_index.csv` record `n_classes` = 10 (`chance_uniform` 0.1) over 10 input positions. `build_index.py` reads `n_classes` and the positions from the probe records, which were written under an earlier BridgeVariable definition with 10 classes at every input position, position 0 included. The current `bridge_variable` (`pipeline/probes/variables.py`) sets `n_classes` to the measured label cardinality and keeps only non-constant, load-bearing positions: for `psum_novel_id__moe8` / `bridge1_novel_id` the zoo row has 10 classes at 10 positions (best accuracy 0.998 at L2), while the showcase record (`results/showcase/05_bridge_psum_novel_id__moe8/probes/psum_novel_id__moe8/bridge1_novel_id/site_scan.json`) has 2 classes at positions 1–9. The verdicts do not use `n_classes` (they compare against the embedding baseline and the majority class), but where the current definition drops a position, the zoo row's position means still include it.

## Conventions

- **Architectures** are the 8 zoo variants, keyed by the model-name suffix (no suffix = Large).
- **Size** of a ContrastCircuit / SuppressionCircuit is its *fringe* (candidate edges minus the base circuit's edges). Edge fractions use the live edge budget (`n_edges_live / n_edges_full`; attention-only models exclude the EAP graph's phantom MLP edges). Size tables keep only models whose checkpoint has its architecture's canonical geometry (escalated checkpoints are excluded).
- Fringe metrics (`d_*`, release, collateral) in rows without a recorded metric unit are token-level deltas (the pipeline's pre-`2026-07-v2` convention).
